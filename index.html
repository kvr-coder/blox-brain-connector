Blox Brain
YouTube Connector

A local Codex connector for the owner of the English, Spanish, and Lithuanian Blox Brain YouTube channels. The current version reads authorized channel details and recent uploads.

Read the privacy policy →

Available now

The owner signs in with Google and grants read-only YouTube access. When asked for a report in Codex, the connector verifies the authorized channel and returns its name, ID, upload count, and a sample of upload titles and IDs.

Planned management tools

As each channel is connected, the planned tools would let the owner update supported channel profile fields, edit video details, manage playlists, upload selected videos as private, and schedule publication. These features would require a separate Google approval for management access.
